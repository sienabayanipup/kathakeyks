Ordering is simple.

  1. Browse the cake gallery.
  2. Choose your preferred design or send an inspiration photo.
  3. Discuss the details and event requirements.
  4. Confirm your order through our official contact channel, with a 50% down payment.

Important reminders for made-to-order cakes

Advance ordering is required

Please order ahead of time. We do not accept same-day or rush orders, so every cake gets proper preparation.

Booking confirmation

Your order is reserved once we have confirmed the details and received payment. A 50% down payment is required.

Customization details

Send your theme, flavor, size, dedication or message, and inspiration photos (if any) to avoid delays.

Cancellations

Because each cake is made just for you, cancellations or major changes may not be allowed once the order is confirmed.

Pick-up and delivery

Please be on time for pick-up, or make sure someone can receive the cake on delivery, to avoid delays or damage.

Subject to availability

Slots may be limited during peak seasons and holidays, so early reservations are highly recommended.
