Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you book. Still have a question? We are happy to help.

Ask Us on Facebook

How far in advance should I book a custom cake?

We recommend ordering your customized cake 2 to 3 days before the scheduled date, so we can validate your order.

How stable are the cakes and cupcakes?

To keep your cake stable, we use dowels for tiered cakes with two or more layers. This prevents sliding, leaning, and collapse.

How do I take care of my cake?

A few simple tips keep your cake fresh and safe:

  • Avoid direct sunlight and keep the cake in a cool, dry place.
  • We recommend refrigerating it at 2 to 5°C, where it will last about two days.
  • Do not use contaminated utensils, to avoid contamination and spoilage.

What payment methods do you accept?

We only accept QR code and e-wallet payments such as GCash. For bank transfers, we only use BPI QR codes.

What are the delivery and pickup options?

We do not accept motorcycles for cake delivery because they could harm the cake in transit, especially tiered cakes with two or more layers. For a safer and more comfortable ride, we recommend a car or any other four-wheel vehicle.

Pickup is available at 9 a.m.
